LinkedIn Ghostwriting

You know you should post on LinkedIn. You don't.

Every founder knows it. LinkedIn is where clients, investors, and talent find you. But you're busy running a company, and writing posts falls to the bottom of the list every single week.

Here's what I do: I write your LinkedIn posts in your voice. Not corporate. Not generic. Not "thought leadership" slop. Posts that sound like you on your best day — sharp, honest, a bit contrarian. You review them, you approve them, you hit publish. I do the writing. You get the visibility.

1

We talk for 45 minutes

I learn your voice, your opinions, your industry, and what makes you different. I ask the questions that pull out the insights you'd post about if you had time. What do you believe that most people in your industry would disagree with? What frustrates you? What are you proud of?

2

I write your first 4 posts

I draft them in a Google Doc, written in your voice — using the phrases you actually use, the opinions you actually hold. You read them. If they don't sound like you, tell me what's off. I fix them. When they sound like you, we're ready.

3

You post, I watch, I adjust

You publish the posts. I track what gets engagement and what doesn't. I adjust the next batch based on what's actually working — not what a "LinkedIn strategy guide" says should work.

4

Ongoing: 8 posts per month

Every month: I write 8 posts, you review and approve, you post. Simple. You spend 15 minutes reviewing instead of 4 hours writing. Your LinkedIn stays active, your voice stays authentic, and you never have to stare at a blank screen again.

What you get

  • 8 LinkedIn posts per month, written in your voice
  • A 45-min onboarding call to capture your tone and perspective
  • Posts delivered in a shared doc — review, tweak, approve
  • Engagement tracking — I watch what works and adjust
  • Topic ideation based on your industry and goals
  • First 4 posts free — if you don't like them, you walk away
