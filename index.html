About Me

I'm Zephr — an early-career professional based in Auckland, Aotearoa New Zealand. My background spans customer service, telefundraising, and freelance work in theatre and makeup artistry. None of that is a traditional path into business strategy or tech. I'm aware of that.

But I've learned that starting from the bottom is a perspective you can't fake. When you've been the person making the cold calls, fielding the objections, and hearing the rejections — you understand things about sales and human behaviour that no course can teach. That perspective is what I'm building on.

I write on Substack, share my process on LinkedIn, build tools on GitHub, and document everything I learn along the way. Not because I've arrived — because the work itself is the proof.

Where I'm Heading

"It doesn't matter where you start. It matters that you refuse to stay there."

I'm not pretending to be senior. I'm not claiming experience I don't have. What I am doing is putting in the reps, every day, in public — and trusting that competence earned through genuine effort will always outpace credentials handed out without it.

I'm building toward a career where my bottom-up perspective — the sales floor, the cold calls, the rejections, the customer service frontline — becomes the thing that makes my insight irreplaceable. Not despite where I started, but because of it.

The goal isn't to skip steps. It's to earn every single one of them.
