Business · Red-team your strategy
The Bullshit Filter
Your pitch deck sounds incredible in the boardroom. Everyone nods. But does it survive contact with someone who's numerate, skeptical, outside your industry, and has no incentive to be polite? I'll red-team your business strategy, value proposition, or investor pitch. Every claim stress-tested: "This checks out" / "I don't believe this number" / "This means nothing to someone outside your bubble." You get the scrutiny your team won't give you because they're invested in the answer being yes, and your investors won't give you because they've already decided. I have no stake in your outcome — I just have a nose for claims that don't survive contact with reality.
You get: Line-by-line teardown of your strategy doc, pitch deck, or value proposition (up to 20 slides/pages). Every claim marked: "Verifiable and compelling" / "I don't believe this" / "This is jargon that means nothing." Performance · From backstage to boardroom
The Performance Review
I spent four years as a professional makeup artist in theatre — backstage at Mary Poppins and CATS, working under pressure with a countdown to curtain. Theatre taught me something most sales and leadership trainers will never understand: what you say matters less than how you deliver it. Pacing. Timing. The pause before the key line. The energy shift when you move from rapport to pitch. The ability to read a room and adjust in real time. Your team's calls and presentations are performances — and right now, most of them are amateur theatre. I'll review recordings through the lens of stagecraft, because the best performers don't just know their lines — they know how to make an audience lean in.
You get: Review of 3 recorded calls or presentations from your team. Each scored on 8 performance dimensions: opening energy, pacing, pause usage, tonal shifts, rapport authenticity, pitch delivery, objection recovery, and close. Each person gets a one-pager: what's working, what's killing the performance, and the one delivery change that would transform their next call.
$550 NZD
