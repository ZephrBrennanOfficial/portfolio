About Me

I'm an early-career professional in Auckland. I write — on Substack, on LinkedIn, in repos, and now for founders who'd rather run their business than stare at a blank LinkedIn post.

My writing style: short, honest, punchy. No corporate jargon. No "thought leadership" that says nothing. I write the way people actually talk — because that's what gets read.

Currently working as a Telefundraiser at Fundamental Worldwide. Background in customer service. Former freelance makeup artist for musical theatre. I document everything I learn publicly.
